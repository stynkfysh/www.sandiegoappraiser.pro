Our Appraisal Services

Specialized valuations for every situation, from family transactions to court proceedings.

Date of Death Appraisals

My core specialty: pinpointing a home's fair market value as of a specific past date so an estate can be settled, reported to the IRS, and its stepped-up basis documented correctly.

Divorce Appraisals

A neutral opinion of value that holds up for both sides — used by San Diego family-law attorneys, mediators, and the court to divide real property fairly.

Estate & Trust Appraisals

Valuations that support probate, trust distribution, and tax reporting — the everyday companion to date-of-death work for executors and trustees.

Bankruptcy Appraisals

An independent value the trustee, the debtor's attorney, and the bankruptcy court can rely on to determine real property equity.

Expert Witness & Litigation Support

A report built to be challenged, and testimony to back it up, when a property's value is contested in court, arbitration, or a tax appeal.

Pre-Purchase Appraisals

An independent number before you sign — so you know what a San Diego home is actually worth before you commit to buying it.

Pre-Sale Appraisals

Price it right the first time — an independent, certified valuation to set your San Diego listing price instead of guessing.

Tax Appraisals

Support for property-tax appeals, gift-tax reporting, and other tax matters that require a documented, defensible value.

Family Transaction Appraisals

A documented fair-market value for sales, gifts, and transfers between family members — the kind of paper trail the IRS expects to see.

Insurance Dispute Appraisals

Objective evidence when you and your insurance carrier disagree about what a property, or a loss, is actually worth.

Bail Bond Appraisals

A fast, credible valuation to verify the equity in real property pledged as bail collateral.

Immigration Appraisals

Independent property valuations documenting real-estate assets for USCIS visa, residency, and sponsorship filings.
